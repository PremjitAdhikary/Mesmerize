As a kid, I played battleship a lot. The first time I played it was at my cousin's place where he had the classic board game. The simplicity of the game blew my mind.

In my school between classes (sometimes during...) me and my deskmate would draw up grids in our rough copies and play. Whispering at each other ... D5 ... Miss ... F2 ... Hit ... . On this Page, I recreate the magic.

Battleship is a two-player guessing game. Each player starts with a fleet of warships and take turns to call shots at the opponent's ships. The objective is to destroy the fleet of ships of the opponent before yours are destroyed.

The game is played on four grids, two for each player. On one grid the player arranges ships and records the shots by the opponent. On the other grid, the player records their shots.

Before play begins, each player secretly arranges their ships on their primary grid. Each ship occupies several consecutive squares on the grid, arranged either horizontally or vertically. The number of squares for each ship is determined by the type of ship. The type and the number of ships are same for both the players.

Here you get play against the computer. Once you have placed your ships you are ready for the battle. You will always call the first shot. Once your turn is over, the computer will take its turn and then return the control to you.

Main Menu

Any Orange element is a button. If it is grayed out, it's disabled. Select the difficulty level you want to play and start one of the games.

Game - Ship Placement

At first, you place your ships. The orange button lets you switch the ship from being horizontally or vertically placed. Once you have decided on the orientation, click the cell on the grid where you want your ship to be placed. If the ship is vertical, the cell you select will be the topmost part of the ship. If horizontal, the cell will be the leftmost part.

If the selected cell cannot fit the ship in the grid, the ship will not be placed and you need to select a different cell. The other rule is the ships cannot intersect.

You get to place all the ships in your fleet from biggest to smallest. Once all the ships are placed, the battle begins.

Game - Battle

When it's your turn, the grid to record your shots will display. Click on the cell you want to target. It will reveal if it was a hit (red cross) or a miss (blue cross). Once your turn is over, the grid to record your opponent's shots will display (with your ships that you have placed). This will show where your enemy has fired. Then it's your turn again. This will continue till you or your enemy has no ships left. That's when it's game over!

On the right the active ships for the player and the enemy will be displayed. Anytime a ship sinks, it will be removed from the list of active ships.

Game Over

When the game is over, the winner will be displayed. An orange Exit to Main Menu button will show up. Click to exit.

Each player starts with the following ships:

  • Aircraft Carrier - 5 x 1
  • Battleship - 4 x 1
  • Destroyer - 3 x 1
  • Submarine - 3 x 1
  • Patrol Boat - 2 x 1

At each turn, the player gets one shot. The fastest one to sink all five ships of the opponent wins.

Each player starts with the following ships:

  • Frigate - 2 x 1
  • Patrol Boat - 2 x 1

At each turn, the player gets one shot. The fastest one to sink both the ships of the opponent wins.
