The basic idea is releasing a bunch of particles undergoing random walk. Let them aggregate over a period of time and watch the beauty unfold.

The clusters formed in DLA processes are referred to as Brownian Trees.

By constraining the walkers, and repeating/reflecting the cluster formed, we have a Brownian Snowflake.
