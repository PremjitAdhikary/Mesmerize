Slim Forest Noir Forest Stylized Forest

Environment demo for platform scrollable.

The background forest is generated based on the Random Tree in Fractal Trees from earlier. But the cost of rendering a forest every frame was high.

So rather than drawing all the trees every frame, they are drawn in an off screen buffer and on every frame render, the buffer is rendered.

For Stylized Forest, the library p5.scribble.js has been used to give a hand-drawn feel to the trees.

Controls

  • Click and hold on the canvas to move.
  • Hold mouse down on left side to move left and on right side to move right.
  • Based on whether the mouse is towards the center of the canvas or the edge, the speed changes.

The background artwork is based on something I saw sometime back.
