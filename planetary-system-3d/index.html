Modeled after our very own Solar System with few liberties:

  • The Size of the planets is not up to scale. Jupiter is almost ten times the size of earth and Sun is almost ten times Jupiter. Not true in this simulation.
  • The distance of the planets from the Sun is also not up to scale, neither is the asteroid belt.
  • The number of moons is not exact.

Using mouse on the Viewport allows to control the camera. Click and drag to move Camera. Scroll to Zoom In/Out.
