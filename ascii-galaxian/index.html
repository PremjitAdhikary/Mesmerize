Galaxian - the first console game I played. This page is a tribute to it.

I came across some awesome ASCII art sometime back and that was the inspiration to make this as an ASCII version. So instead of drawing on a canvas with p5js, I went all vanilla. The viewport is a grid of divs where the characters are rendered.

Few pointers:

  • There are different classes of Alien ships. Each with their own attack and armor rating.
  • The Alien ships progressively get better armor every wave. Which means you will have to hit multiple times to destroy ships at higher levels.
  • There is a kill streak and levels at which bonuses will be unlocked to make your Galaxip more powerful. You get hit and the streak is reset.
