This is for me to balance the game. For anyone else a bit mathematically inclined, you may use this to strategize.

Points to note:

  • Every character has 2 attacks - Primary and Secondary
  • Whenever a character attacks, the damage to the opponent is a function of the attacks innate damage and strength of the character
  • Strength is affected by a multiplier which is dependent on difficulty for NPCs and on power-ups for Hero
  • For every attack, the damage is randomly set in the range of its minimum and maximum
  • Whenever a character gets attacked, the damage it takes is reduced based on the armor the character has
  • Similar to Strength, Armor is affected by a multiplier which is dependent on difficulty for NPCs and on power-ups for Hero
  • If hero defends while attacked, the damage dished is halved.
